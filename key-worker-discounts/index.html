How verification works

In the US most brands verify through ID.me or SheerID: you prove your status once (with documents or an official login) and the discount is applied at checkout or sent as a code. In the UK, schemes such as the Defence Discount Service, Blue Light Card and Health Service Discounts collect discounts for the armed forces, emergency services and NHS staff; each has its own eligibility rules.

Group discounts usually can't be combined with promo codes, so compare which gives the lower total.
