How student verification works

Brands don't check your student status themselves. They use a verification service, and you sign in or upload proof once:

  • UNiDAYS and Student Beans: free student-discount apps used by many fashion and lifestyle brands (Gymshark uses Student Beans).
  • SheerID: verification built into the brand's own checkout (Nike uses it in the US).
  • ID.me: a US identity service used for student, military and key-worker discounts.

More detail in our guide: student discount verification explained.

Tips

  • Student codes are often single-use and expire quickly (Nike's expire two weeks after issue), so generate one when you're ready to buy.
  • Education stores for laptops and phones (Dell, Samsung) can save more than any public code.
  • Many student discounts can't be combined with other codes.

Frequently asked questions

Can high school students get student discounts?
Sometimes. Nike's US student discount covers high school, college and university students aged 16+, and Dell includes high school students 16 and older. Others are for college or university students only, so check each offer's terms.
