Voucher code, promo code or coupon: what's the difference?

They are the same thing with different names. In the UK and Europe people usually say voucher code or discount code; in the US it is a promo code or coupon code. All of them are words or strings you type into the checkout to get money off, free delivery or a gift.

We also list offers that need no code: deals (price drops or free shipping), free gifts added in the basket, and member perks such as student, military or sign-up discounts.

How to use a code

  1. Tap Get code on an offer to reveal and copy it.
  2. Go to the store, add your items and check they meet the terms (selected items, minimum spend, new customers…).
  3. Paste the code into the promo or discount box at checkout and make sure the total drops before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

How often are these codes checked?
Each offer shows the date we last saw it on the brand's site. Codes without an end date are hidden after 10 days unless we re-check them, deals and gifts after 14, and long-running member perks after 45.
Can I use more than one code?
Usually not: most stores allow one code per order, and group discounts (student, military) often can't be combined with promo codes. Check the terms on each offer.
