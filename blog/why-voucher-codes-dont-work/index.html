Why voucher codes don't work, and how to fix it

You paste the code, hit apply, and get "this code is not valid". Usually the code is fine; one of its conditions isn't met. Here's the checklist.

By the VoucherVault editorial team · 16 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Clay Banks on Unsplash

The eight usual suspects

  1. Selected items only. Codes like "20% off selected" skip anything not in the promotion, so your total may not move.
  2. Minimum spend. Some gifts and codes need a spend before or after discount; check which.
  3. New customers only. First-order codes (such as ASOS NEWHERE) check your account and sometimes your address or card.
  4. App vs website. Some codes only work in the app (ASOS HEYAPP) or only online.
  5. Wrong region. A US code won't work on the UK store and vice versa.
  6. Can't be combined. Group discounts (student, military) and sale prices often exclude other codes.
  7. Typos and spaces. Copying can add a space; codes may be case sensitive.
  8. It has ended. Brands can end codes early, especially when stock runs out.

What to try next

  • Open the offer's terms and check each condition against your basket.
  • Remove sale or excluded items and try again.
  • Type the code by hand.
  • Look for a no-code alternative on the same store: member perks, free shipping thresholds or gifts.
  • Ask the store's customer service: they can confirm whether a code is live.
