Cyber Monday 2026: how to use promo codes without wasting them

Cyber Monday falls on Monday 30 November 2026, three days after Black Friday on 27 November. Many brands run extended sales across the whole weekend, so codes are everywhere and many stop working fast. A little preparation means the code you grab actually takes money off. Here is the checklist we use.

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Cyber Monday 2026 is Monday 30 November. Black Friday is Friday 27 November. Many stores in the US, UK and Europe start deals earlier in the week and run them past Monday, so check each brand’s own site for its actual end date. See our Black Friday and Cyber Monday page for brands with offers we have seen live.

Before the day: build a shortlist

  • Write down the 3 to 5 items you really need, with the normal price today. Our guide to checking a Black Friday price shows how to spot an inflated “was” price.
  • Sign up to the brands’ own email or app lists: many offer a first-order discount, and some send early access links. See whether sign-up discounts are worth it.
  • Check student, key-worker or military schemes: our student discounts and key worker discounts pages list verified programmes.

Read the terms before you click apply

Most failed codes fail on the same few rules. Look for these on the offer’s own terms:

  • Selected items only: sale items, new arrivals and some brands are often excluded.
  • Minimum spend: the discount may only apply above a basket value.
  • Stacking: many stores allow only one code per order, and some do not allow codes on already discounted lines.
  • New customers only: sign-up codes can be single use per email or account.
  • Region and end date: a US code may not work on a UK store, and sale codes often end at midnight local time.

Our post on why voucher codes don’t work goes through the common errors. We check each offer on the brand’s own site and show the date we last saw it live; see how we check.

Use codes from sources you can trust

Fake coupon pages and fake “gift card” messages spike around big sales. Go to the brand’s own site or app, or to a source that shows where each code came from, and never pay to “unlock” a code or share payment details to claim one. Our guide to spotting fake coupon scams lists the warning signs. For live offers, see hot deals and expiring soon.

Know your rights if you change your mind

If you buy online in the UK, you normally have 14 days to cancel an order and a short period afterwards to send goods back, subject to exceptions such as some made-to-order or sealed items (GOV.UK, as of November 2026). Retailers’ own return windows may be longer at Christmas, so check each store. Read Black Friday returns and refund rights before you buy, and keep your order confirmation and the code terms until the return window ends.

Frequently asked questions

When is Cyber Monday 2026?
Monday 30 November 2026, the Monday after Black Friday on 27 November.
Can I use two promo codes on one order?
Often not. Many stores allow one code per order, and some exclude sale items. The terms on the brand’s offer page say what applies.
Why does my promo code say invalid?
Common causes are an expired code, a minimum spend, excluded items, a region mismatch or a new-customer-only rule. Re-read the terms and check the end date.
Can I return something bought with a code?
Usually yes, under the retailer’s returns policy and UK distance-selling rules. A refund normally reflects what you actually paid after the discount.
