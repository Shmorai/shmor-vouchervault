What makes a deal "hot"?

We flag an offer as hot when it gives an unusually large saving for that brand, works sitewide or across a big range (such as all clearance), or stacks on top of sale prices. It is an editorial pick, not a claim about how many people used it.

A big percentage isn't always the best saving: a 20% code on full-price items can beat a 50% sale on things you don't need. Compare the final basket total.
