Is an ending-soon deal worth rushing for?

Only if you were going to buy anyway. Countdown timers are designed to hurry you, and many brands run similar offers again within weeks. Before you buy, check the price you'll actually pay against other stores and the item's usual price.

The end dates here are the ones the brand publishes. Times are often midnight UK time or the store's local time, so don't leave it to the last minute.
